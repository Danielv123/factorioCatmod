# Sound credits

The cat sounds are cut from recordings on [Freesound](https://freesound.org), all released under
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain). `tools/sounds.py` downloads them and cuts
the clips; thanks to everyone who shared them.

`cat-die.ogg` has been in the mod since 2018.
